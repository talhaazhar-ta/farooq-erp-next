## Rules (each gets a test)
- **Search:** server-side, on generated columns plus `fold_search`, the same engine as S4 / S8. Every word must be found. It looks at the number (and its compact form), supplier (as printed and as it is now), supplier invoice no., vehicle, driver, warehouse, line products, and the amount forms. A typed date is a filter.
- **Filters and sorts:** as listed above. The cards cover the whole filtered list.
- **CSV:** the columns above, BOM, injection guard.
- **Print (one model):**
  - **fix 4**: show ordered and received bags separately, never ordered labelled "Bags received";
  - A4, one page, paper colours in any theme;
  - Urdu snapshots.
- **View page:** header, lines (ordered / received / returned), payments with links, the supplier's balance.
  - The Edit button is drawn from `actions.edit`, disabled with the server's reason.
  - The cost block appears only with `PROFIT_VIEW` (absent otherwise, not null).
- **Access:** reads need any of `PURCHASE_CREATE | TRANSACTION_CORRECT | FINANCIAL_REPORT_VIEW` (confirm against the legacy page guard). INVENTORY gets 403 unless the legacy showed purchases to the warehouse role; check it and record the answer.
