## Planner decisions after S11 (binding — don't reopen without a reason written into STATUS)
These answer S11's five open legacy behaviours (listed in "What S11 actually built") and settle what the draft left open.
1. **Stock movements on create:**
   - one `PURCHASE_IN` per product × warehouse with received > 0;
   - `unit_cost_p` = the **received-weighted unit price** of that pair's lines, rounded (with one line, that is its unit price — the legacy value, which `carriedCost` ignores anyway);
   - dated the purchase date;
   - `source_type` `PURCHASE`, `ref_type` `PURCHASE`.
2. **Stock movements on edit:**
   - the **net difference** per product × warehouse, like S7's invoice edit: `PURCHASE_IN` for more bags received, `PURCHASE_REVERSAL_OUT` for fewer;
   - `ref_type` `PURCHASE_EDIT`, dated the purchase date **as saved by this edit**;
   - `unit_cost_p` as in decision 1 on an IN, NULL on an OUT (the importer stores the legacy 0 as NULL).
   - This differs from the legacy's full reverse + re-add; the level and the purchase ↔ stock check come out the same. Record it under "Different from the legacy".
   - A pair whose net is 0 posts nothing, even if its price changed. The average is rebuilt from the lines (decision 3), not from movements.
3. **Average cost after every save** (create or edit), for every product × warehouse the purchase touches now **or touched before the edit**, via `weightedAverage` over all non-cancelled purchase lines of that pair, on the `readProfitCostBasis` basis:
   - **A number** → write `avg_cost_p`. Also write `last_cost_p` = the saved line's unit on the basis (goods, or landed = goods + round(share ÷ q) + round(operational ÷ q)).
   - **`null` (no received bag anywhere for the pair), and this purchase has a line in the pair → port the legacy fall-back:** `avg_cost_p` = `last_cost_p` = that line's unit on the basis. With several lines in the pair, use the last by `sort_order`, as the legacy loop does.
     - Why port it: 10 of 16 real stock rows got their bags only through warehouse-app receipts (`ADJUSTMENT_IN`), and the office rule is to enter the bill with **Received = 0**. The legacy fall-back is how those bags get the bill's price as their cost. Without it they would stay on the carried cost.
     - This is the one case where the average is set without a received line. The S11 check lists such a row as "kept from before"; the bridge asserts the value explicitly.
   - **`null` and the pair was only dropped by this edit → leave the row alone** (legacy: "if nothing is left, the old average is kept").
   - Never nudge from the previous figure, never touch a row this purchase does not touch, and never touch the `damaged` bucket.
4. **Per-line cost columns on every save:**
   - `goods_unit_cost_p` / `charge_share_p` / `landed_unit_cost_p` come from `allocateCharges` (fix 3 included). The charges are `freight_p + loading_p + other_charges_p`.
   - `landed_unit_cost_p` = the allocated landed unit + round(`operational_share_p` ÷ received), which is the legacy 26 wrapper folding the operational share back in.
   - **`operational_share_p` is carried over unchanged** on a kept line (M6 owns it). A new line gets NULL.
5. **`costHistory` is not written** (store deferred; M4/M6). **Part delivery:**
   - S12 writes the **fixed** goods unit (line value ÷ ordered);
   - the bridge mirrors part deliveries on the legacy JSON with the fixed figure and says so;
   - an imported line keeps what the backup says until that purchase is next saved.
6. **Status on every save** = RECEIVED / PARTIALLY_RECEIVED / ORDERED from Σ received vs Σ ordered. Imported DRAFT / CANCELLED purchases:
   - an imported **CANCELLED** purchase cannot be edited (`A cancelled purchase cannot be edited.`);
   - an imported **DRAFT** one may be edited, and it becomes one of the three statuses on save (the legacy had no drafts, so a save always recomputes).
7. **Header money on save:**
   - `discount_amount_p` = item discounts + the overall discount (the combined legacy figure);
   - `subtotal_p`, `tax_p` and the charges come from `invoiceTotals`;
   - `total_qty_milli` = `ordered_qty_milli` = Σ ordered, `received_qty_milli` = Σ received;
   - `stock_applied` = Σ received > 0.
8. **Payout core:** extract `writePayout(tx, actor, {supplierId, amountP, method, reference, date, note, allocations})` from `PaymentsService.pay`. Both `pay` and the purchase save call it, and S3's payment tests stay unchanged.
   - The voucher reference = `supplier_invoice_no` (the builder's own reference field was dropped by the legacy — keep that).
   - The method defaults to `Cash`.
9. **Read permissions** for `GET /purchases/:id` and the pickers: any of `PURCHASE_CREATE | TRANSACTION_CORRECT | FINANCIAL_REPORT_VIEW`.
   - INVENTORY and SALES get 403 until S13 confirms the legacy page guard; if it differs, S13 changes it and says so.
   - The cost columns and averages appear only with `PROFIT_VIEW` (absent, not null).
10. **Owner-visible changes to list in STATUS** (for the owner's review):
    - paid > total or negative refused on a new purchase;
    - a line discount above the line amount refused;
    - the part-delivery cost fixed;
    - net movements instead of reverse + re-add (the stock history shows fewer rows);
    - `costHistory` not kept.

  cost columns `goods_unit_cost_p`, `charge_share_p`, `landed_unit_cost_p`, `operational_share_p` (**nullable: NULL = never computed; 0 is a real value** — a cancelled landed cost leaves 0), `batch_no`, `notes`, `legacy_doc`.

- **The importer resolves `receivedQty`:** absent in the legacy document = the whole quantity, `0` = nothing arrived; S12 always writes the resolved number.

**Legacy behaviours the draft above does not mention (decide them in S12, write the answer here).**
1. `PURCHASE_IN` movements carry `unit_cost_p = unit price` (not the landed unit), dated the purchase date; the edit's reversal is dated the ORIGINAL purchase date (the fixture's `PUR-2026-000001` second line shows it).
2. The legacy save wrapper (`17-profit.js`) updates a line's stock row even when the line received nothing: `avgCostP = weightedAverage(...) || useUnit` and `lastCostP = useUnit`, so an ORDERED bill (received 0) with no other received purchase of that product × warehouse sets the average to its own unit cost. The S11 check would list such a row as "kept from before" (no received line), not fail it. Decide: port the fall-back, or leave the average alone when nothing was received (simpler; then say so in the owner-visible list).
3. The legacy `costHistory` rows written on every save are not ported (store deferred).
4. A **part delivery** stored by the legacy carries the OVERSTATED goods unit (the fixture's `PUR-2026-000004`: 1,667 a bag; the fixed figure is 1,000). The importer keeps what the backup says; S12's save writes the fixed figure; the reconciliation reports lines where the two differ as informational (`averageCost.allocation.differs`), so a bridge that mirrors an operation on the legacy JSON must use the fixed figure for a part delivery and say so.
5. `total_qty_milli` is the ORDERED total; status = RECEIVED when Σ received ≥ Σ ordered, PARTIALLY_RECEIVED when 0 < Σ received < Σ ordered, ORDERED when nothing arrived (`02-services.js` 1166).

## User decisions (2026-09-25 — enforce, test, don't reopen)
1. **Average cost is maintained here.** After every save, recompute `stock_levels.avg_cost_p` / `last_cost_p` for every product × warehouse the purchase touches **or used to touch** (dropped lines).
   - Use S11's `weightedAverage` with the settings basis (`LANDED` in the real data).
   - `null` (nothing received) keeps the old average. That is the legacy rule for a dropped product (`17-profit.js` wrapper).
   - `last_cost_p` = the line's unit on the chosen basis.
   - Store `goods_unit_cost_p` / `charge_share_p` / `landed_unit_cost_p` on each line (`allocateCharges`), and **keep `operational_share_p`** as it is. It belongs to M6's landed costs; the legacy 26 wrapper folds it back into `landed_unit_cost_p`.
   - **Never nudge** the average from the previous figure: always recompute from every purchase line. `test-stock-value.mjs` M5: a later typed receipt cost does not override a purchase-kept average.
2. **Permissions:**
   - create = `PURCHASE_CREATE` (OWNER, MANAGER);
   - edit = `PURCHASE_CREATE` **or** `TRANSACTION_CORRECT` (legacy `canEdit`, `02-services.js:1019-1022`);
   - a payment made with the purchase also needs `PAYMENT_PAYOUT` (403, and the whole save is refused);
   - INVENTORY and SALES: none.
3. **Legacy bugs fixed:**
   - `paid` below 0 or above the total is refused on a **new** purchase, too. Reuse the legacy edit wording for "more than the total".
   - A line discount above the line amount is refused, with a new message in the legacy style.
4. No drafts, no cancel, no delete, no separate Change supplier, no `receiveMore`.

## Rules (each gets a test)
- **Create.** One transaction:
  - number `PUR-<business year>-<6>` via `nextNumber` (the prefix comes from the settings, default `PUR`);
  - lines with stable ids and snapshots;
  - status ORDERED / PARTIALLY_RECEIVED / RECEIVED from Σ received vs Σ ordered;
  - `PURCHASE_IN` per product × warehouse with received > 0, `unit_cost_p` = unit price as in the legacy, `source_type` PURCHASE, dated the purchase date;
  - **Received = 0 posts the bill only**, with no stock;
  - the average cost (decision 1);
  - one PURCHASE journal entry (`purchaseLines`);
  - audit `Purchase recorded`.
- **Payment with the purchase.** If `paid > 0`, write one PV voucher through **one** payout core shared with `PaymentsService.pay`. Extract `writePayout(tx, …)` the way S7 extracted `writeReceipt`, and leave S3's tests unchanged. The voucher gets:
  - the note `Paid with purchase <number>`;
  - reference = the supplier invoice number (legacy);
  - the purchase date;
  - one allocation to this purchase, capped by S3's rules.
- **Edit.** Stale revision refused (the invoice wording pattern). Everything in `editErrors`, verbatim:
  - a returned or landed line cannot be removed or re-producted;
  - received cannot go below returned;
  - supplier locked by a payment or a return;
  - paid cannot be lowered;
  - paid above the total refused;
  - **net stock guard** per product × warehouse (honours `allowNegativeStock`).

  What an edit does:
  - **Stock moves by the net difference** per product × warehouse. The legacy did a full reverse and re-add; the net is the same. `PURCHASE_IN` / `PURCHASE_REVERSAL_OUT`, `ref_type` `PURCHASE_EDIT`. Pick one dating rule and justify it: the legacy dates the reversal on the original date and the re-add on the new date.
  - The one journal entry is rewritten in place (amount and date).
  - Raising paid → one extra voucher for the difference.
  - Line ids stay stable; dropped lines are deleted, unless they carry returns or landed costs, which is refused.
  - The average is recomputed (decision 1).
  - Audit `Purchase edited`, with before and after.
- **Idempotency** through `request_keys` (kind `PURCHASE`). **Concurrency:** lock order purchase → supplier → stock levels (sorted). Two edits of one revision → one wins; two creates racing → consecutive numbers.
- **Reads:** `GET /purchases/:id` returns header, lines, payments and allocations, the stock effect, and `actions {edit}` with a reason. Cost fields are shown only with `PROFIT_VIEW`. The supplier pickers (products mapped to a supplier first is **not** ported — `supplierProducts` is deferred; say so) and the last purchase rate per product.
- **Payment reverse** refreshes nothing stored: the payment status is derived. Prove a reversed PV lowers `paid` on `GET /purchases/:id`.
