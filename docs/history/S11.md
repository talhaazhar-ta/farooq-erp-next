# S11 — M3 part 1: purchase lines, full header, average cost (data model, import, reconciliation)

**Date:** 2026-09-25. **Plan:** `docs/sessions/S11.md` (final). Data / import / reconciliation only — no service, no API, no screen.

## What was built

- **`packages/shared/src/purchase-cost.ts`** (+ `purchase-cost.test.ts`, 16 tests): `allocateCharges` (`Cost.allocate`, **fix 3**: goods unit = line value ÷ ORDERED bags; charges per received bag, `received || ordered`),
  `unitCostForAverage` / `weightedAverage` (`Cost.weightedAverage` for the PURCHASE basis, `Landed.weightedAverage` for LANDED: goods + round(charge share ÷ q) + round(operational share ÷ q), q = received; `null` when nothing was received), `costBasisOf`, `DEFAULT_COST_BASIS` (`LANDED`).
- **Migration `0008`** (`0000`–`0007` untouched): `purchases` gets the full header (snapshots, `warehouse_id`, delivery, `subtotal_p`, `discount_amount_p` (the legacy's ONE combined figure), `tax_p`, freight / loading / other, `total_qty_milli`, `ordered_qty_milli`, `received_qty_milli`, `line_count`, notes, description, `stock_applied`, `migrated`, `revision`, `created_by`, `updated_at`, `client_op_id`), a partial UNIQUE index on `purchase_number`, an amounts CHECK; new **`purchase_items`** (godown per line, `qty_milli` ordered > 0, `received_qty_milli` ≥ 0 not capped, `returned_qty_milli`, money, nullable cost columns `goods_unit_cost_p` / `charge_share_p` / `landed_unit_cost_p` / `operational_share_p`, `legacy_doc`; CHECKs: quantities, money, **discount ≤ gross**); hand-appended `REVOKE TRUNCATE` + comments saying who writes each cost column (S12: goods / charge / landed; M6: operational share).
- **`packages/db`**: `ledger.ts` `PURCHASE_SOURCE`, `purchaseLines`, `purchaseMemo`, `purchasePosts` (the importer now uses them; its journal is unchanged — 35 entries / 9,305,000 on the fixture, statements proof unchanged); new `settings.ts` `readProfitCostBasis(db | tx)` / `costBasisFromDoc` (missing = `LANDED`).
- **Importer**: `purchases` header fully mapped; `purchaseItems` moved out of `DEFERRED_STORES` with a field classification (`operationalShare` included; `landedCosts`, `landedCostExpenses`, `inventoryCostAdjust`, `costHistory`, `supplierProducts` stay deferred, the first two of those three are READ by the reconciliation). `receivedQty` **absent = the whole quantity, 0 = nothing arrived** (stored resolved, raw kept in `legacy_doc`). Fails loudly on: dangling purchase / product / warehouse, a discount above the gross, a 4-decimal quantity, a fractional paisa, a negative received quantity, a duplicate line id, a duplicate purchase number, an `orderedQty` that differs from `quantity`, a line with no godown at all, any unclassified field. `loaded` / audit row now carry `purchase_items` / `migratedPurchases`; the database check is now "migrated through 0008".
- **Reconciliation** (`reconcile-purchases.ts`, three new checks + report lines, exit non-zero on any mismatch): (1) **purchase totals** recomputed from the lines with `invoiceTotals` (overall discount = header discount − Σ line discounts, legacy `toDraft`); (2) **purchase ↔ stock** per purchase × product × warehouse (a cancelled purchase must net 0; migrated ones skipped and listed); (3) **average cost** = `weightedAverage` of the non-cancelled lines vs every `stock_levels.avg_cost_p` with a received purchase line behind it (basis from the settings), rows without one LISTED as "kept from before"; plus the operational share = the landed-cost rows in the backup (a cancelled landed cost adds nothing) and landed unit = goods + charges + operational; informational: stored goods unit / charge share vs the fixed `allocateCharges`.
- **Fixture** (`build-fixture.ts`, hand-computed in the header comment "Purchases (S11)"): the four purchases keep totals / dates / suppliers / balances; PUR-1 two lines in two godowns + freight 40,000 + loading 20,000, edit history on both lines, second line `receivedQty` absent + a landed cost (operational 9,000), first line's landed cost cancelled (operational 0); PUR-2 DRAFT with line + overall discount and nothing received; PUR-3 CANCELLED (+6 −6); PUR-4 part delivery (100 ordered, 60 received, the legacy's overstated 1,667 stored); 2 new stock rows (p-2@wh-2, p-3@wh-2) and 6 new movements; three inventory rows with no received purchase line (kept from before).

## Deviations from the plan / decisions

- **The fixture's PUR-4 is now `PARTIALLY_RECEIVED` (was `ORDERED`), PUR-2 has a line + overall discount (unit 50,000)** so the discount split has a hand case; totals, dates, suppliers and every balance are unchanged. Existing hand-counted stock numbers (rows 5→7, movements 24→30, 212.5→275.5 bags, `p-1@wh-1` average 79,000→64,286) were updated in `invoice-lines-stock`, `invoice-stock-safety-net`, `invoice-stock-fail-loudly`, `import-fixture` and `invoices-ledger-bridge` (41 / 39 movement docs). S8's synthetic 300-invoice backup empties the fixture's purchase lines / landed stores (they point at the fixture's products).
- **e2e dataset:** `e2e-pur-1` stays header-only (no lines). Giving it a received line needs a stock movement and level in the e2e dataset that the M2 stock specs are tuned to; S13 (list / view) is the first screen that needs purchase lines and adds them then. The reconciliation lists it as "no lines" (not failed).
- **`orderedQty` ≠ `quantity` aborts the import** (not in the plan; the legacy always writes the same figure in both and nothing else could explain a difference). A line with no godown falls back to the header's (the legacy migration's `l.wid || pu.wid`); with neither it aborts.
- **`received_qty_milli` is not capped at ordered by a CHECK** (plan: check the real data and say what you chose): no real line has received > ordered (0 of 6), but the legacy allowed raising the ordered quantity on a full load, so a cap could refuse a valid old backup.
- The `readProfitCostBasis` rule is repeated in `@farooq/db` (it does not depend on `@farooq/shared`); `purchase-lines-stock` proves the two agree on every value.
- The `REVOKE TRUNCATE` in migration 0008 changes nothing by itself (the default privileges never granted TRUNCATE); it documents the rule and `purchase-db-rules` proves it.

## Findings

- The planner's numbers held on both real backups (6 of 7 averages match, the 7th kept). **Fix 3 changes no real figure**: 0 of 6 real lines differ from the fixed allocation (no real part delivery, no real charges).
- The legacy save wrapper (`17-profit.js`) sets a line's average to its own unit cost even when it received nothing and no other received purchase exists (`avgCostP = weightedAverage(...) || useUnit`). The check lists such a row as "kept from before". Left for S12 to decide (written in `docs/sessions/S12.md`).
- Postgres evaluates CHECK constraints in name order: a negative quantity with a price fails `purchase_items_discount_chk` before `…_qty_chk` (the db-rule tests use unit price 0 to isolate the quantity rule).
- One mutation survived on the first sweep (M4: weighting the average by ordered instead of received bags — no test had a line with received ≠ ordered next to another line): a test was added and the mutation is red. M19 (dangling warehouse) first went red for the wrong reason (it broke the valid fixture import), so it was redone as M19b.
- The first full run failed two api files that use the fixture: S8's synthetic backup inherited the fixture's purchase lines (dangling products) and the S7 bridge hard-coded 35 / 33 movement docs. Both fixed; the second full run is the one recorded.
